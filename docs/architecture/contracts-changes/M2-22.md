- **M2-22:** The zip reader's names and codes (layout 4.5, ADR-0037 decision 1). Two codes are new in 10.3 and in the catalog (`catalog/errors/RRS-E.json`): `RRS-E5007` for a zip that is cut short or disagrees with itself (a truncated central directory, a bad offset, two entries sharing archive bytes, a checksum mismatch, a damaged or short deflate stream), and `RRS-E5008` for a zip Rastai doesn't read (encryption, several disks, another compression method). Neither fits the existing `RRS-E5001` to `RRS-E5006`. Both messages carry a `{reason}` placeholder, filled with the reason the reader gives. `RRS-E5002` also covers a deflate stream that holds more output than its entry declares, which layout 4.5 specifies. 15.1 registers the names the reader builds: `list_zip_entries`, `ZipEntry` (`path`, `compressed_bytes`, `uncompressed_bytes`, `method`, `data_offset`, `crc32`), `ZipMethod`, `copy_zip_entry`, `read_zip_entry`, the zip errors, and in `zip/inflate.rs` `InflateStream`, `new_inflate_stream`, `inflate_chunk` and `InflateProgress`. `ZipSource` is registered as the writer's input (M2, built by M2-20) so the name stays with the writer. `miniz_oxide` stays pinned in the workspace table, and its `deny.toml` wrapper row and `check-deps` row already existed.
